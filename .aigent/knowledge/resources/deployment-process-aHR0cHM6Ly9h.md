# **For React Changes**

## 1. Start
The process begins with the initiation of a change request.

## 2. Deployment on SIT
- The change is deployed on the** SIT** environment.
- The change is marked as **“Ready for QA”**.

## 3. QA Verification on SIT
- QA verifies the change in the SIT environment.
- Verification continues until the change is fully fixed and aligned with requirements.

## 4. Mark Change as Ready for UAT
- Once QA validation is complete on SIT, the change is marked as **“Ready for UAT”**.

## 5. Deployment on UAT
- The change is deployed on the **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** environment.
- The change is marked as **“UAT”**.

## 6. QA Verification on UAT
- QA verifies the change on UAT.
- Verification continues until the change is confirmed to be fully fixed and meets requirements.

## 7. Mark Change as Ready for Production
- After successful UAT verification, the change is marked as **“Ready for Prod”**.

## 8. Deployment on Production
- The change is deployed on the **Production** environment.

## 9. QA Verification on Production
- QA verifies the change in the Production environment.
- Verification continues until the change is fully fixed and working as expected.

## 10. Mark Change as Done
- Once QA confirmation is complete, the change is marked as **“Done”**.

## 11. End
The process concludes successfully after marking the change as complete.

# **For Hot Fixes:**

## 1. Start
The process begins with the initiation of a bug on production.

## 2. Deployment on SIT
- The bug fix is deployed on the** SIT** environment.
- The bug ticket is marked as **“Ready for QA”**.

## 3. QA/PM Verification on SIT
- QA/PM verifies the bug fix in the SIT environment.
- Verification continues until the bug fix is fully fixed and aligned with requirements.

## 4. Mark Change as Ready for UAT
- Once  QA/PM validation is complete on SIT, the bug ticket is marked as **“Ready for UAT”**.

## 5. Deployment on UAT
- The bug fix is deployed on the **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** environment.

## 6. Mark Change as Ready for Production
- Without verification on UAT, the  bug ticket is marked as **“Ready for Prod”**.

## 7. Deployment on Production
- The bug fix is deployed on the **Production** environment.

## 8.  QA/PM Verification on Production
-  QA/PM verifies the bug fix in the Production environment.
- Verification continues until the bug fix is fully fixed and working as expected.

## 9. Mark Change as Done
- Once QA confirmation is complete, the  bug ticket is marked as **“Done”**.

## 10. End
The process concludes successfully after marking the bug fix as complete.
