# Purpose
Establish a clear and collaborative process for creating Root Cause Analysis (RCA) documents in order to:

- Identify the root cause of incidents or defects.
- Improve communication between teams.
- Maintain accountability and visibility throughout the investigation process.

# 1. Kick Off RCA Document
The RCA process begins once an issue has been identified as requiring formal investigation.

Team Members should collaborate respective to their Role.

### Tech Lead
- Create the RCA document, if the Tech Lead isn't available, he should assign someone to perform this task (PM as fallback).
- Share the document with all relevant stakeholders.
- Define the Scope of the investigation.
- Assign the main contributors involved in the RCA process.

### Primary Member(s)
- Leading the technical investigation.
- Providing technical context.
- Assisting in identifying the root cause.
- Gathering evidence and logs.
- Proposing corrective actions.

### Secondary Member(s)
- Assisting with testing and validation.
- Providing supporting context.
- Reviewing findings and contributing feedback.
- Helping validate proposed solutions.

### QA Member(s)
- Providing testing context and reproduction steps.
- Identifying the testing scope and affected scenarios.
- Confirming whether the issue was previously covered by manual or automated tests.
- Determining if the issue was an edge case or a missing test scenario.

# 2. RCA Alignment and Follow-Up
- The RCA owner and contributors should:
- Review gathered information collaboratively.
- Identify gaps in the investigation.
- Assign follow-up tasks when additional validation is required.
- Ensure action items have clear ownership.
- Confirm that all impacted areas have been reviewed.

- Updates regarding the RCA status should be communicated periodically to:
- Project Manager / Tech Lead
- Relevant teams
- Relevant stakeholders

- The integration of a collaborative meeting is recommended for the contributors to discuss RCA Alignment.

# 3. Review and Delivery
The RCA draft will be reviewed collaboratively by all involved members. 

The review process should validate:

- Accuracy of findings.
- Clarity of the root cause explanation.
- Completeness of corrective actions.
- Alignment between teams.

## Final Approval
The final RCA document will be reviewed and approved by:

- Project Manager / Tech Lead
- Additional stakeholders

## Delivery
Should be complete in 1-2 hours max.

Once approved:

- The RCA document will be shared with the appropriate teams and stakeholders.
- Action items will be tracked until completion.
- Relevant learnings may be added to internal documentation or knowledge bases.
